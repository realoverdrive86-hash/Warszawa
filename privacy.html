🇬🇧 English

1. General Information
The mobile application "Warszaw with kids" is an informational guide and interactive map of child-friendly places in Warsaw.

2. Data Collection and Usage
We respect your privacy. The application does not collect, store, or share any personal data, registration details, or usage analytics on external servers.

3. Location Data (GPS)
If you grant permission to access your device's location, your coordinates are processed strictly locally on your device to display your current position on the map. Location data is never transmitted to us or third parties.

4. Third-Party Services
The app uses open map tiles (OpenStreetMap / Leaflet) to render the city map, which may process standard technical network requests (IP address) required to load map images.

5. Contact Us
